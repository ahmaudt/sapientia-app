Sapientia — iOS — appearance

Light and dark

Dark mode inverts the two grounds the system already owns: the paper becomes the ink. Nothing new is introduced. The steel field stays reserved for the rite — onboarding, the scan, the prayer — so it still reads as a different kind of screen in either mode.

Instagram is set aside until you tap your tag.
In code this replaces the fixed .preferredColorScheme(.light) at the app root with a stored setting (Light / Dark / System) and turns the flat tokens in SapientiaTheme into appearance-aware pairs. The ritual views keep setting accent-900 explicitly, so they are unaffected. Corner registration marks and hairlines take the reversed divider — on the dark ground they need the extra two percent to stay visible.
